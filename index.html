BEHIND THE SCORE

What 58% positive actually means.

The percentage is positive reviews divided by all counted reviews at capture time — cumulative, not release-day only. Steam's off-topic filter is applied; a store page with narrower language or purchase filters can legitimately show a different score.

Reviews explain themselves only if you read them. The score summarizes recommendations; it cannot tell you which trade-offs — monetization, servers, combat — would matter most to you.

QUESTIONS BEHIND THE NUMBERS

FAQ.

What was AION 2's Steam launch-day peak?
SteamDB recorded 397,905 concurrent players on October 5, 2026. We identify this as the launch-day record; we do not invent an exact peak time beyond what the source publishes.
When did AION 2 fully launch on Steam?
The full release opened October 5, 2026 at 13:00 UTC. Advanced Access began September 30, 2026. See the NC release announcement and the official Steam page under Methodology.
Does this include PURPLE players?
No. The Steam player-count endpoint measures players active through Steam. It does not cover NC's PURPLE platform, so the true cross-platform population is higher.
Is concurrent player count the same as daily players?
No. Concurrent players are people playing at the same time — different from unique players over a day or month, owners, or paying users.
Do these numbers prove commercial success?
They show Steam activity only. Revenue, costs, conversion and cohort retention are separate measures that cannot be calculated from this count alone. AION 2 is free to play.

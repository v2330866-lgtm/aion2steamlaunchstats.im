READING THE COUNTER

What this number can and cannot tell you.

Concurrent players are people in the game through Steam at the same moment. It is not unique players, downloads, accounts, or revenue. Online games have daily rhythms — evenings, weekends, maintenance and patches all move the line, so compare similar hours rather than launch night versus a quiet morning.

AION 2 also runs on NC's PURPLE platform, which this endpoint does not see. Treat the Steam count as a floor on the game's population, never a total. For how snapshots are captured and what happens when a request fails, see the methodology.
